Frequently asked questions

Is listing really free?

Yes. Adding and keeping listings is free. Every listing is verified by our team before it goes live.

How does bidding work?

In bidding categories each lead opens a short auction. Bids start at the floor price, are sealed, and are weighted by your rating and response time. Up to 3 top vendors win the lead.

What do winners pay?

Just above the next-highest bid — never more than your own bid, never less than the floor price. If nobody else bids, you pay the floor.

Do customers see bids?

No. Bidding is for vendors only. Customers simply receive replies from up to 3 matched, verified providers.

Which categories use fixed prices?

Lower-value, high-volume categories such as Brand & Printing, Office Equipment & Supplies, Delivery & Logistics and Consulting & Funding unlock at a fixed price — no bidding.

What if a lead is fake?

Report it within 48 hours. Wrong numbers, spam and duplicates are refunded to your wallet after review.
