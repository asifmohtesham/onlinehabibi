PRODUCT SPEC

Users, roles & permissions

Three account types — Platform, Vendor and Customer — each with its own dashboard. Every vendor company gets a separate, isolated workspace with its own team, leads, wallet and payouts.

Lead lifecycle

1. Customer submits request (login required)2. OTP-verified → Lead created3. Admin/auto verification4. Sealed auction among vendors with verified matching listings (or fixed price)5. Top 3 win — pay just above next bid6. Quote sent7. Customer accepts → Order + escrow8. Delivery approved → Payout (minus commission)9. Review

Data isolation rules

  • Every record carries a vendor_id or customer_account_id; queries are always scoped to the signed-in user’s workspace.
  • Customer contact details stay masked until a vendor unlocks the lead; unlocks are logged and charged.
  • A lead is shared with at most 3 vendors (configurable). Vendors never see each other’s quotes.
  • Sales Agents see only leads assigned to them; reassignment is by Owner or Manager.
  • Platform staff actions are written to the audit log with user, role, time and record.
  • Super Admin is the only role that can create admin users or change commission and lead prices.
