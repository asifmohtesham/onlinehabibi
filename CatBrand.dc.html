Tips before you order

  • Always approve a digital proof showing your logo’s size, colour and position before production.
  • Embroidery suits polos and caps; screen printing is best value for large T-shirt runs.
  • Shop signage in Dubai usually needs a municipality permit — ask your provider to include it.
